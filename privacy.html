Privacy policy

This page explains which personal data is processed when you visit margaritacolombo.com or contact me, why, and what your rights are. It follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where it applies to you, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Who is responsible

Margarita Colombo, Zollikon, Switzerland, is responsible for the processing of personal data on this website. For any question or request about your data, please contact me by email.

Visiting this website

The website is hosted by Vercel Inc., USA. When you open a page, Vercel’s servers automatically process technical data needed to deliver the site and keep it secure: your IP address, the date and time of the request, the page requested, the referring page, and your browser and operating system. I do not combine this data with other information to identify you.

Fonts, images and styles are served from this website itself. No fonts or other assets are loaded from third-party servers.

Analytics cookies, only with your consent

If you choose “Accept” in the cookie banner, this website uses Google Analytics 4, a service of Google Ireland Limited, to understand how the site is used. Google Analytics then sets cookies (_ga and _ga_QPVLEL4L8Q, stored for up to two years) and collects usage data such as pages viewed, time on page, the referring website, approximate location, and device and browser type. Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses. Usage data is kept in Google Analytics for a maximum of 14 months.

If you choose “Decline”, Google Analytics is not loaded and no analytics cookies are set. You can change your choice at any time through “Cookie settings” in the footer. Declining removes existing analytics cookies from this site.

Your choice is saved in your browser’s local storage so the banner does not appear on every visit. It stays on your device and is not sent to me.

Contacting me

If you write to me by email, I process your name, email address and the content of your message in order to reply and to follow up on your enquiry. I keep this correspondence only as long as it is needed for that purpose or as required by law.

Links to other websites

This website links to LinkedIn and other external websites. These are ordinary links: no data is sent to those services until you click. Once you do, their own privacy policies apply.

Legal bases

Where the GDPR applies, analytics cookies are based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Delivering and securing the website and answering enquiries are based on legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) or on steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Transfers abroad

Vercel and Google may process data in countries outside Switzerland and the European Economic Area, including the United States. Such transfers rely on the providers’ certification under the Swiss-U.S. and EU-U.S. Data Privacy Frameworks or on standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

You can ask for information about the personal data I hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to or restrict its processing, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. I do not sell personal data and do not use it for automated decisions.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In Switzerland this is the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC). In the EU you can contact the authority in your country of residence.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy when the website or the law changes. The current version is always available on this page.
